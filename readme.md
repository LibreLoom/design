# Our Company's Logo and Other Related Things

**Refer to this page for our style and branding guidelines.**

### We use the Simplex Mono design language.

Simplex Mono design guidelines:

- Color Pallet:
  - Black: #000000
  - White: #FFFFFF
  - Grey (UI accent): Anywhere between #000000 and #434343 depending on the accent level
- Typography:
  - Headings: FreeMono Standard-Weight (or similar monospace font)
  - Main Body Text: Noto Sans Standard-Weight(or similar sans-serif font)
  - Accents: Use italic single or two word emphasis. Use bold for entire line emphasis.
- Overall UI design:
  - Flat design with lots of padding (enough to look not compressed but also doesn't feel big)
  - Minimal VFX & shadows
  - Circle/pill/extended circle UI elements
  - Navigation/toolbars float, and go on the *bottom*. They are collapsible to hamburger on mobile.
  - Nice, fluid animations. Take inspiration from Apple's Liquid Glass on this one.
